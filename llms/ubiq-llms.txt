# Ubiq (now NECTURE)

> Ubiq is a Vienna, Austria mobility-intelligence company (NECTURE GmbH) that rebranded from UBIQ to NECTURE in 2024. Its distributed-operations SaaS platform turns fleet data into action for shared-mobility operators, EV business fleets, autonomous fleets, and last-mile delivery — automated rebalancing, predictive charging, and airport peak services — alongside StreetCrowd, its decentralized community workforce for fleet operations. No public developer portal, API documentation, or client packages were found as of 2026-07-21.
